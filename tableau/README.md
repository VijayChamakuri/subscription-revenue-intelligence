# Tableau Scope Decision

Tableau is intentionally omitted from the current implementation. Power BI owns the governed executive and operational decision experience, and duplicating it in Tableau would add maintenance without a distinct business purpose.

A future Tableau artifact is justified only if it delivers a separately validated customer-growth investigation story for Marketing, Product, or Customer Success, with acquisition quality, onboarding, adoption, retention, expansion, and churn exploration that is materially different from the Power BI pages. Until that experience can be authored, tested, and demonstrated, no workbook or published-link claim should appear.
