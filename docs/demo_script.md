# Demonstration Script

## Before the demo

Run the documented pipeline and test commands, record the run identifier, verify all certified tie-outs, and open the real Power BI report. Do not use screenshots or results from an unverified refresh.

## Ten-minute demonstration

### 0:00 to 1:00: Business framing

Introduce the fictional B2B SaaS setting, stakeholder questions, synthetic-data disclosure, and single-source-of-truth objective.

### 1:00 to 2:00: Architecture

Show the architecture diagram and explain each technology's single responsibility. State the measured event volume and benchmark environment only if verified.

### 2:00 to 3:30: Revenue movement

Open the MRR movement page. Reconcile opening to closing MRR, isolate the largest verified movement segment, and drill into one synthetic account's lifecycle evidence.

### 3:30 to 5:00: Finance control

Open the billing and revenue reconciliation page. Trace one invoice through payment or refund and revenue schedule. Show a known injected exception and the rule that catches it.

### 5:00 to 6:30: Retention and risk

Compare logo and revenue retention without mixing them. Show cohort size and censoring. Explain the churn validation window, probability calibration, threshold cost framework, and one account's noncausal risk drivers.

### 6:30 to 7:30: Growth economics

Compare channel CAC, retention, and gross-margin-adjusted payback. Call out attribution and LTV assumptions.

### 7:30 to 8:30: Forecast and scenarios

Show candidate backtests, selected forecast, intervals, and a scenario control. State clearly that the scenario is modeled opportunity, not realized impact.

### 8:30 to 9:15: Metric trust

Open the data-quality page, confirm refresh status, test results, MRR bridge, and dashboard-to-warehouse tie-outs.

### 9:15 to 10:00: Close

Present no more than five verified recommendations, each with evidence, affected segment, action, modeled range, difficulty, limitation, and monitored metric. End with the most important production-data limitation.

## Recovery paths

If Power BI is unavailable, demonstrate warehouse outputs and mark BI execution as pending. If a test fails, show the failure and stop using affected metrics. If a finding cannot be reproduced, omit it rather than substituting an estimate.
